Business WhatsApp number

This number is printed in your messages (as {contact}), on invoices and on appointment confirmations, so patients know where to reach you. Example: +977 970-2235789.

Good to know: a website cannot choose which WhatsApp account sends a message. Messages are sent from the WhatsApp that is open on your phone or computer, so open your business WhatsApp there (WhatsApp Business app, or WhatsApp Web signed in to that number). This setting makes sure patients are told to reply to the business number. Sending automatically from the number, without you pressing send, needs WhatsApp's Business API (a paid Meta service).
